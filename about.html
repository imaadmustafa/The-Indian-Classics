The Story of Indian Motoring

India's automobile history is filled with remarkable cars that became part of everyday life. From the legendary Hindustan Ambassador and Premier Padmini to rugged Mahindra Jeeps and elegant European-inspired sedans, these vehicles represent different chapters of India's journey on four wheels.

About The Indian Classics

The Indian Classics is a digital collection dedicated to India's vintage and classic automobiles. Our goal is to preserve the stories, designs and memories associated with the cars that shaped generations of Indian motorists.


Explore legendary manufacturers, discover forgotten classics and learn about the automobiles that became symbols of different eras in India's history.
